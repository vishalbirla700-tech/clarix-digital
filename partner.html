✦ Common Questions

FAQ for Partners

Is it free to become a Clarix Partner? +
Yes, 100% free. No registration fee, no monthly charge, no target to hit. Just sign up and start sharing.
Do I earn for free sign-ups too? +
No. Earnings are only credited when your referred user upgrades to a paid plan (Creator, Pro, or Agency). Free sign-ups are tracked in your dashboard but do not generate earnings. This keeps the program sustainable and the payouts meaningful.
How does referral attribution work? +
When someone clicks your referral link (clarix.digital/?ref=YOURCODE), their browser saves your code. When they sign in to Clarix, your code is permanently recorded to their account. Last-click attribution — whichever link they click last before signing in gets the credit.
When and how do I get paid? +
To qualify for a monthly payout, your earnings must reach ₹100, you can request a payout from your partner dashboard. Enter your UPI ID, and our team will manually process the transfer within the 15th of the following month. There is no automated payment system yet; all payouts are reviewed and approved by Clarix team.
Can I refer anyone — from any city or state? +
Yes! There is no geographic restriction. Share with anyone in India or abroad. Clarix supports 20+ languages and works globally.
What tools do I get to help me sell? +
Your partner dashboard includes: a personal CRM with full client and earnings view, a Product Knowledge Hub with sales scripts and objection handling, pre-built WhatsApp and Telegram share messages, and a copyable referral link. Everything you need to close.
Is there a limit on how much I can earn? +
No cap whatsoever. 10 Pro clients = ₹500/month. 100 Pro clients = ₹5,000/month. The more you refer, the more you earn.
